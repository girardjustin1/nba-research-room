# Independent audit: NBA Research Room engine

Audit date: October 5, 2026 (America/New_York; October 6 UTC). Scope: the supplied checkout and cleaned DuckDB store. No network research or data fetching. No production code, tests, configuration, jobs, or source database were changed.

**CONFIRMED** means an executable counterexample, data query, or benchmark reproduced the stated behavior. **SUSPECTED** means a reasoned methodological concern whose effect has not been reproduced. Reproducing a score is not evidence that its evaluation is leakage-free.

## 1. Summary

1. **The reported benchmark headlines reproduce, but that does not validate the experiments.** Weekly Brier, daily-news Brier, add/drop win rates, and both requested teammates-out scoreboard columns match to rounding. Doubtful/Questionable/Probable play rates also match, with slightly different denominators. `make test` has 263 passes and five Chrome-startup errors. The remaining findings explain why reproduced scores should not yet be treated as prospective performance estimates.
2. **The news replay does not evaluate morning forecasts using morning information.** Almost all selected report rows for the replay arrive later that day. Sunday acquisition decisions also use Monday-midnight feature states. These timing errors prevent interpreting the replay as a prospective test of the stated decision policy.
3. **The optimizer can emit an invalid roster even though its MILP solved optimally.** Converting internal roster transitions into moves discards unpaired drops. In the first three real replay weeks, **2/21 accepted plans hold 13 players under the configured limit of 12**; a small CBC counterexample isolates the lost-drop mechanism. The solver can also recommend re-adding a player while its own feasibility checker rejects the plan.
4. **Several probability calculations are invalid or inconsistent.** Teammate adjustments predict more free throws made than attempted in 234 held-out player-game rows, producing negative percentage variances. Completed 4–3–2 category wins receive 0% week-win probability, and the forecast path uses a different correlation model than the headline.
5. **The numerical experiments are development evidence, not a clean final validation set.** Settings and model choices were repeatedly selected using 2025–26. The weekly and news harnesses also draft different leagues. Preserve an untouched evaluation period, a fixed roster universe, and explicit decision timestamps before using small score differences to choose production behavior.

### Scope and interpretation of the logic review

**Availability and teammate fitting.** `BaselineModel` implements the zero/played mixture correctly: for conditional mean μ and variance v, E[Y] = pμ and Var(Y) = pv + p(1−p)μ². Dispersion is fitted to projected minutes, not actual minutes. The teammates OLS uses realized historical absences on the training seasons; that is a valid supervised training covariate when the deployment version receives a pre-decision estimate. It does not automatically leak test labels. The `who sat` column intentionally supplies held-out outcomes as inputs and must stay labeled hindsight. The production no-news column reproduced its near-zero effect. The coefficients reproduce approximately 0.3703 for points, 0.3537 for assists, and 0.03495 for rebounds. Minutes and missing-share deltas are clipped; adjusted minutes propagate to counting stats before minutes caps.

**“Not listed” roster construction.** The news replay avoids assigning everyone to the current `players` table's teams. It creates player-game candidates by joining that season's daily states to the team schedule, requires the team to have filed, and applies the rotation/recent-play-rate rule only to unlisted candidates. Players need an earlier row before entering the daily state universe. However, the candidates' team IDs come from the forward join in F03; using the season's own box scores does not make team membership causal.

**Calibration.** `calibrate` fits the baseline, variance multipliers, and category correlation using seasons before the test season. Held-out outcomes score coverage; they are not directly used to estimate those multipliers. Calibration inherits F03's state/roster problem and the chosen hyperparameters. It uses in-sample training-season residuals of the fitted adjustment, rather than temporal out-of-fold residuals; possible spread underestimation from that choice is unquantified. Current fitted points dispersion is 3.3863, consistent with the reported 3.39. With current settings, independently rerun 2025–26 80% coverage ranges from 77.97% to 82.17%, close to the documented 78–82%. The 6,000 sampled teams are heavily overlapping synthetic draws, not 6,000 independent observed team seasons.

**Probability approximations.** Counting variances add, and each category multiplier scales variance once (SD by its square root). Turnovers and their residual correlation have the correct sign. Percentage means are ratios of summed makes/attempts, not averages of percentages. That formula is appropriate only for a valid stat line; F18 shows that teammate adjustments can violate it. Their variance is a fixed-expected-attempt binomial approximation; it omits random composition/attempt uncertainty, partly absorbed by fitted multipliers. Correlated weekly scoring is Monte Carlo, not an “exact” probability: defaults use 1,000 shared draws. The optimizer's pivotal-category derivative assumes independent categories and holds spreads fixed, although final scoring uses correlation; it is a surrogate search, not a proof of optimal week-win probability. The 0.002 acquisition penalty is applied to that surrogate; the final acceptance test merely checks positive total simulated improvement, so it does not enforce a measured 0.2-point gain for every individual acquisition.

**Acquisitions and replay accounting.** An add's earliest effective calendar date is correctly computed as decision date plus one day. The re-plan loop commits only moves effective on the next played date, carries the resulting roster forward, and subtracts only actual committed additions from `left`; uncommitted future recommendations are not counted. It includes only prior days' realized totals when solving today's plan. However, the report cutoff, Sunday cutoff, roster extraction, and re-add eligibility defects above remain. The news arm applies official report overrides only to today, starts with the same no-news Sunday plan, and does not replay the multi-day X/BDL absence logic. Plans reset to original draft rosters each week; opponents never stream; free-agent competition between independently replayed matchups is not modeled. IL occupants stay fixed; the MILP does not search IL transfers/direct IL adds. Realizations in the weekly benchmark set lineups with perfect knowledge of who actually played. That is explicitly implemented hindsight, not equivalent to an injury report for late scratches or unexpected DNPs; the news replay's projection-based lineup policy is a different experiment.

### Independent statistical checks

- **On versus off weekly Brier:** on minus off = **+0.007025** (worse). Independent 10,000 paired-matchup resamples give 80% [-0.022567, +0.036863], 95% [-0.039089, +0.052550]; resampling the 20 whole weeks gives 95% [-0.044965, +0.057341]. This does not establish equivalence. Because the model also changes the draft, it measures a combined procedure, not the teammate adjustment on identical matchups. The documented 80% interval [−0.029,+0.037] is broadly close but does not reproduce exactly under half-tie scoring; original scratch code is unavailable.

- **Pooling and dependence:** Brier is averaged over available days within each matchup-week and then across matchup-weeks. Short NBA weeks therefore receive more weight per forecast. Equal-forecast-weight Briers are monday=0.1102273, daily=0.1069771, daily_news=0.1050287. Each version is scored against its own lineup-dependent final result; the pair is a policy comparison, not always the same target. The 10,000-resample checks use seed 318; production uses 2,000 resamples with seed 0. Week clustering is a sensitivity check and does not eliminate repeated-roster dependence; it need not widen every interval.

- **Large streaming gains remain narrow in scope:** the weekly off run improves by 39.643 points (production 80% interval [34.286, 44.643]). These are synthetic matchups against non-streaming opponents, with weekly roster resets, imperfect historical eligibility, hindsight lineup reactions in the weekly run, and confirmed illegal returned plans. ## 4. Not verifiable with this data

- **Kalshi and sportsbook benchmark claims:** 40 games/1,665 rungs, Briers 0.164 versus 0.179, 80% ranges, points/rebounds/assists RMSE 7.75/2.96/2.48, market bias, liquidity and name-match rates. `props_ladder` has zero rows. Scratch experiments are not included. Moreover, the documented old test compares conditional market prices to `P(plays) × P(exceeds | plays)`, while the later note says inactive props are void/fair-settled. That scoring definition needs a matched played-game/settlement audit; its numerical effect cannot be checked here. Synthetic overlay math/gating tests do not validate these empirical superiority claims.
- **2024–25 report calibration and pooled status fitting:** the supplied reports cover 2025–26 only. The original 45,643-row two-season analysis, “not listed” play-rate bins fitted on 2024–25, and the exact original report-only/report-plus-unlisted scoreboard columns cannot be independently reconstructed as originally fitted. BBM blend, draft timings/ADP pool claims, actual Yahoo roster/move legality, real season win odds, and the live projection/weekly-scoreboard claims cannot be measured. Backtest rosters are synthetic, not the user's historical Yahoo league.
- **Archived news/ingest behavior:** `status_events`, `api_responses`, and `props_ladder` are intentionally empty. Live X parsing accuracy, freshness at actual polling times, source outages, and real market/news-gate impact are unverified. NBA parser unit fixtures run, but original PDF bytes/raw API bodies are unavailable for an independent reparse of the matched rows.
- **Superseded development experiments:** the older 80-matchup runs, shrinkage/tempering searches, context-model/LightGBM ablations, random-team correlation comparison (0.1982 versus 0.1967), and several draft/UI/API timings are described but their original scratch scripts/configuration snapshots are absent. The empty tables are intentional cleaning, not ingestion failures. `table_counts.json` records all table counts; the copy retains 138,296 game logs, 104,756 advanced-stat rows, and 21,633 NBA report player rows.

## 5. Things that looked wrong but turned out fine

- **The new teammate prior columns are shifted correctly.** `teammates.prior_states` computes actual missing-team context after a played game, forward-fills it, then shifts once by player. All 12 `tmo_prior_*` columns remained unchanged when same-game DNPs were tampered with, both in a synthetic example and a real game. `test_leakage.py` discovers them dynamically through `feature_columns`, so the basic truncation tests do include them; it is the broader as-of/roster coverage that is deficient.
- **Actual teammate absences in training do not themselves prove held-out leakage.** Training/test seasons are separated. The production prediction path derives current absence weights from `1 − p_play`, not from `y_did_play`. The audit supplied actual test DNPs only for the explicitly labeled hindsight scoreboard.
- **Baseline and market mixture algebra is correct.** Conditional 20 ± 5 with p=0.4 becomes mean 8 and SD 10.295630; converting back returns exactly 20 ± 5. Out players stay at zero, one-line market fitting retains conditional SD, the probit fit has the right sign, and the cap/news gate excludes explicitly older limiting-news quotes. F09/F10 concern which quote/news timestamp is selected, not this algebra.
- **Weekly projection rows are schedule-expanded rather than restricted to future appearances.** `backtest.week_projections` uses pre-cutoff players and their team's full schedule. Its existing future-removal test passes. It is safe relative to its actual Monday cutoff; F02 concerns claiming the plan was made Sunday.
- **Variance units and turnover signs are consistent.** Category multipliers apply to variance, not SD, and are not inadvertently applied twice to completed totals. FG%/FT% means use summed makes and attempts.
- **Core acquisition-budget accounting is coherent.** MILP rising roster transitions require add variables, whose sum is bounded by acquisitions left. The re-plan loop subtracts committed additions once and skips optimization when none remain. Re-add policy and emitted-roster validity are separate defects; they should not be described as an automatic fifth-acquisition bug.
- **Paired replay summaries really are paired.** `backtest_news.summarize` and `summarize_moves` pivot by week/team before differencing. The news bootstrap first averages the days within a matchup-week. It does not treat every day's forecast as an independent bootstrap observation. The remaining dependence and selection concerns are F15.
- **Existing pre-tip projection grading excludes later runs.** `live_scores.graded_rows` uses `run_at < tip_utc`. This source selection is correctly tested. Empty live tables prevent a real-data performance audit, F13 concerns aggregation after selection; F16 concerns which forecasts enter that selection.
